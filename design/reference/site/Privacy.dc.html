Privacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Last updated [LAST UPDATED DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("Zebrasplit", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the Zebrasplit mobile apps, the website at zebrasplit.com, and any related services (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your phone number, name, and optional profile photo and Venmo username.
  • Trip and expense information: trip names, dates, photos, expenses, amounts, who paid, how costs are split, nights stayed, notes and comments.
  • Receipt photos: images you upload so we can read the total and attach the receipt to an expense.
  • Information about people you invite: the name and phone number of anyone you add to a trip.
  • Communications: messages you send us, such as support requests.

Contacts

If you allow access, the app reads your device contacts so you can pick people to invite. We don't upload or store your contact list. We only receive the contacts you choose to invite.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, we collect device and usage information such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, crash reports, and how you interact with features. We use cookies and similar technologies on our website for basic operation and analytics.

What we don't collect

We don't collect bank account numbers, card numbers or your Venmo password. When you settle up, the app opens Venmo with the recipient, amount and note filled in. The payment itself happens in Venmo and is governed by Venmo's own terms and privacy policy.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: create trips, calculate balances, work out the payments that settle a group, and prepare Venmo links.
  • To verify your phone number and keep your account secure.
  • To send invites, reminders, nudges and other notifications you'd expect from the app.
  • To read totals from receipt photos you upload.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate with you about the Service.
  • To understand how the Service is used, fix bugs, and improve features.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents, and to comply with the law.

3. How information is shared

We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • With people in your trips: members of a trip can see your name, profile photo, Venmo username, the expenses you add, receipt photos on those expenses, your Done status, and your balance in that trip.
  • With service providers: companies that help us run the Service, such as cloud hosting and database providers, SMS and notification providers, receipt-reading (AI) providers, analytics, and crash reporting. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • With Venmo, at your direction: when you tap Pay or Request, we pass the recipient, amount and note to Venmo to start the transaction.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or Zebrasplit.
  • In a business transfer: as part of a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent: in any other case where you ask us to share.

4. Receipt photos and AI

When you scan a receipt, the image is sent to a third-party AI provider to extract the total. We don't use your receipt photos to train AI models, and we don't permit our provider to do so. Receipt photos are stored privately, visible only to members of that trip, and deleted when the trip is deleted.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Closed trips stay in your Past list so you can look back at them. When you delete a trip or your account, we delete the related information within [30] days, except where we need to keep it for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes. Expenses you added to a shared trip may remain visible to other members in anonymized form so their balances still add up.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and update: you can view and edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete: you can delete your account from Settings in the app, or by emailing us.
  • Contacts and notifications: you can turn off contacts access and push notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to opt out of non-essential texts. We'll still send login codes when you sign in.
